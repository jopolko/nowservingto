Indian isn't a monolith and the licence registry knows it. The 68 kitchens below span Hyderabadi biryani houses on Davenport, Punjabi dhabas in Scarborough, Tamil-South Indian dosa counters on Kennedy, and the Indo-Chinese hybrid spots Toronto invented twice over - with 3 of them registered in just the past 30 days. Brampton and Mississauga get the press; the registry shows the city proper is keeping pace.
