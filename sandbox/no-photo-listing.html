What to order

Dhaka chaap kebab, beef hari kebab, butter chicken, beef bhuna, dhaka beef tehari.

Inside the convenience store of a Petro Canada at 427 Kennedy Rd, the kitchen that until last summer served Tim Hortons sandwiches now turns out Bengali rice plates and halal kebabs. Owner-operated, no chain affiliation, no review press. The kind of opening the licence stream catches before the food critics do — and the kind the diaspora reader in Scarborough is actually looking for.
