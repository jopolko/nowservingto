Bhaat N Bytes

Bangladeshi · Scarborough · 427 Kennedy Rd

FIRST SEEN 10 MO AGO Bangladeshi ›

Inside the convenience store of a Petro Canada at 427 Kennedy Rd, the kitchen that until last summer served Tim Hortons sandwiches now turns out Bengali rice plates and halal kebabs. Owner-operated, no chain affiliation, no review press.

What to order

Try the dhaka chaap kebab, beef hari kebab, butter chicken, beef bhuna, dhaka beef tehari.
