/trends magazine hero — no-photo variant

What it KEEPS

Editorial weight, story-led framing, cuisine identity via color, "What to order" as the visual hook, click-through CTA pattern. Mobile-stacks cleanly.

What it LOSES

The emotional "ooh look at the storefront" first impression. For spots where the actual photo isn't compelling (Petro Canada exterior, Star Glow Boba strip mall), losing it is almost an improvement.

OG card — no-photo variant (what X / Slack / iMessage would show)

Honest read

  • For story-driven spots (Bhaat N Bytes, the gas-station Bangladeshi joint), the typographic hero is arguably more compelling than a wide Google street-view shot of the parking lot.
  • For visually-strong spots (a beautiful restaurant interior, a striking storefront), the photo version still wins. Trade-off is real.
  • The OG card survives the photo loss particularly well — at the size X actually shows cards in feed (~500×260), big typography + cuisine color reads better than a small low-detail photo.
  • Where it weakens most: hero strips of 6 cuisines/restaurants. Without photos those become boxed text blocks — could work with strong color blocks, but the information density drops.
  • Compounds with strong recommendations: if a no-photo build paired the typographic hero with rich "next listing" surfaces ("other gas-station kitchens," "other Bangladeshi this month"), the page becomes a discovery engine rather than a portrait. Different aesthetic, similar engagement.
