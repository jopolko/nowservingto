# Community directory submission cheat sheet

Auto-generated from `data/community_directory_candidates.json`.
Re-run `python tools/build_submission_cheatsheet.py` after each spider sweep.

**Total candidates: 43 across 13 cuisines.**

## Workflow

1. Pick a cuisine section below.
2. Pick the highest-confidence directory you haven't submitted to.
3. Open the **Submission URL** in a tab.
4. Copy-paste each field from the table - 2 min per submission.
5. Paste the **Reciprocity ask** into a Notes field, or email the moderator after submitting.
6. Paste the **After submitting, log it** row into `data/community_submissions.md` and fill the date.
7. When the directory confirms reciprocity (or you see the listing live), add a `community_partners` entry to `tools/data/cuisine_intros.json` under that cuisine - next cron renders the footer link.

## Submission etiquette

- **Don't submit the same cuisine to the same directory twice.** Check `data/community_submissions.md` first.
- **Don't auto-submit.** These are community-moderated; reputation cost > time saved.
- **Personalize when the form has a 'Why are you listing?' field.** A one-liner about serving the diaspora specifically beats the templated description.
- **Reciprocity is normal.** Most community directories appreciate being linked back from your /cuisine page footer. Mention it.
