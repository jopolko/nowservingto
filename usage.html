Per-bot row shows hits in the last 7 days · 30 days · last-seen. "Hits" is HTTP requests, not unique URLs - a single bot fetching the sitemap + 50 listings counts as 51 hits. Bots with zero hits are omitted. The "Search engines" section only counts HTML-page crawlers; image / video / news variants (which feed separate indices) are excluded entirely. Requests to common exploit-scanner paths (/.env, /wp-admin/, etc.) are bucketed under "Bot farms" so they don't inflate the legitimate counts. Generated daily from /var/log/apache2/nowservingto-access.log.

These are our internal estimates from a per-call ledger (tools/cache/usage_log.jsonl). Authoritative billing lives in each provider's dashboard: Google Cloud Billing, Anthropic Console, Jina dashboard, X developer portal.
